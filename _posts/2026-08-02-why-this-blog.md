Most engineering blogs are tutorials. This one isn't going to be that.

I want to write about the other half of the job that the part that never
makes it into a README. Standups that go sideways. Retros that actually
change something (and the ones that don't). What it's like to be the
person who has to say "we're not shipping Friday." How a team's culture
shows up in the small decisions nobody writes down.

If you work on a dev team, some of this should feel familiar. If it
doesn't, maybe it'll be useful anyway.

More soon.
